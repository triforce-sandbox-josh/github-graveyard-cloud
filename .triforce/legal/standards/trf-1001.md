# TRF-1001 Fair Commerce (fictional, for the Triforce demo)

TRF-1001 is a made-up internal standard. Triforce checks every change against it, and legal maintains it: this folder, `.triforce/legal/`, belongs to the legal team.

## §1 Billing transparency
- §1.1 Before a visitor commits to pay (a tip, a donation, a sponsorship), the screen shows the total including tax.
- §1.2 The app never tells visitors when they will be charged ("once", "monthly", "free trial") unless the payment flow actually charges that way. Charge-timing copy needs legal approval.
- §1.3 The main payment button says what happens ("Tip $3"), never only "Continue".

## §2 Payment data minimisation
- §2.1 The app stores no card data. Payment providers hold it; the app keeps at most a provider reference.

## §3 Analytics and logging
- §3.1 Analytics events and logs never include raw error text from payment providers, tokens, card data, emails or names.
- §3.2 Any new analytics event that describes a person's behaviour needs the visitor's analytics consent to be checked first.

## §4 Accessible payments
- §4.1 Payment controls have accessible names, and errors are announced to screen readers.
- §4.2 Colour is never the only signal of an error or of a destructive action.

## §5 Third-party code
- §5.1 New runtime dependencies must use a license on the approved list (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-2/3-Clause, ISC).
