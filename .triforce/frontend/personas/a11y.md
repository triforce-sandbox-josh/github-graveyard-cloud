Check new or changed UI for accessible names on controls, keyboard focus order and visible focus styles, colour-only signals, motion that ignores `prefers-reduced-motion` (the ghost cursor, the digging animation), and screen-reader announcements for loading and errors. Check that empty, loading and error states exist and say something useful.
